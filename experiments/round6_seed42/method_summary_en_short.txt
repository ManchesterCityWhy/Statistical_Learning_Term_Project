Real cells plus real later-stage nuclei of the same assay; no model is trained. (1) 80% of the submitted cells (3,200 of 4,000) are E10.5 cells of the official P3 validation release, drawn uniformly at random without replacement from its heart-proper 10x library only (the one library of the three in which at least 90% of the cells carry a heart-lineage annotation; barcode suffix _3); their expression values are unchanged. Reason: the organisers' Data Protocol states that the E12.5 hearts were dissected with most of the surrounding tissue removed. (2) 20% (800 nuclei) are real nuclei of a public wild-type E14.5 mouse heart profiled with the same assay (10x Multiome, Cell Ranger ARC 2.0.2; GEO GSE272154, sample GSM8394736 only), kept if they had at least 1,000 UMIs, at least 500 genes and at most 25% mitochondrial UMIs, normalised like the challenge data and then harmonised against the E10.5 heart library: for each gene, the part of the log-ratio of mean counts (E10.5 heart library / anchor) that four lineages share in sign (smallest absolute value, clipped at e^3) is removed and each nucleus is renormalised. This removes mostly technical differences, and with them changes that all lineages undergo together. Settings were chosen on local proxies built from E8.5, E9.5, E10.5 and the external sample, under conditions written down before the confirmation halves were scored; the choice of the base is a judgement resting on the Data Protocol, not a locally validated result. No leaderboard score entered the content of the prediction. No external data staged after E9.5 up to and including E13.5 and no pre-trained model were used. AI involvement: the method was developed and run with AI coding agents (Claude Code) under the team's direction; the team chose which file to submit. Code is available on request.
