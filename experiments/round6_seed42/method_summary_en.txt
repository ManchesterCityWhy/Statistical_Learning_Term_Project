Real cells of the last observed heart library, mixed with real later-stage nuclei of the same assay.

(1) Base, 80% of the submitted cells (3,200 of 4,000). Cells of the E10.5 stage of the official P3 validation release, taken from its heart-proper 10x library only (the one library of the three in which at least 90% of the cells carry a heart-lineage annotation, counting cardiomyocytes, endocardium/endothelium, cushion mesenchyme, epicardium, blood cells and macrophages: 94% in that library against 47% and 43% in the other two; 11,818 cells, barcode suffix _3), drawn uniformly at random without replacement; their expression values are submitted unchanged. Reason: the organisers' Data Protocol states that the E12.5 hearts were "dissected more specifically, with most of the surrounding tissue removed", and the other two E10.5 libraries consist mostly of surrounding tissue. No cell-type re-weighting, no expression shift and no bulk trend is applied (this replaces the composition deconvolution of our first submission).

(2) Future anchor, 20% (800 nuclei). Real nuclei of a public wild-type E14.5 mouse heart profiled with the same assay as the challenge data (10x Single Cell Multiome, gene-expression part, Cell Ranger ARC 2.0.2, mm10; GEO series GSE272154, sample GSM8394736 "RNA Wild Type e14.5 rep1"; only this sample of the series was downloaded). Nuclei called by Cell Ranger were kept if they had at least 1,000 UMIs, at least 500 detected genes and at most 25% mitochondrial UMIs, mapped to the official gene list by symbol, normalised to 10,000 counts and log1p-transformed, and drawn uniformly at random without replacement.
Before mixing, the anchor nuclei are harmonised against the E10.5 heart library, because the two laboratories' nuclei differ in ways that are not development (several-fold less cytoplasmic mRNA such as ribosomal-protein transcripts, ambient RNA that was not removed, a single-sex pool). Each anchor nucleus is assigned to its nearest E10.5 cell type by correlation with the E10.5 cell-type centroids and grouped into four lineages (cardiomyocyte, endocardial/endothelial, cushion mesenchyme, epicardial). For every gene the log-ratio of mean counts per 10,000 (E10.5 heart library / anchor) is computed per lineage; where all four lineages agree in sign, the gene's counts in every anchor nucleus are multiplied by the exponential of the smallest absolute lineage log-ratio (clipped to a factor of e^3), and the nucleus is renormalised to 10,000 counts. Only the part of the difference shared by all four lineages is removed. That shared part is mostly technical, but it also contains genuine changes that all lineages undergo together (for example the loss of embryonic globins and lower expression of growth-related genes at E14.5), which are removed with it; differences between lineages are kept, and zeros stay zeros.

Choice of settings. All settings were chosen on local proxies built only from E8.5, E9.5, E10.5 and the external sample, each target split into a development half and a confirmation half; the conditions under which this file would replace our previous candidate were written down before the confirmation halves were scored. The anchor share is 0.4 times the elapsed share of the interval between the source stage and the anchor (E12.5 lies halfway between E10.5 and E14.5, hence 0.20). The factor 0.4 was chosen on development halves only, as the value with the smallest worst-case loss over three ways of weighting three groups of local evidence that disagree about the best share: proxies that predict E10.5 from E8.5 or E9.5 favour a larger share, an E8.5-to-E9.5 proxy favours a small one, and scenarios without elapsed time favour none. The choice of the heart-proper library as the base rests on the Data Protocol statement quoted above and on local proxies without elapsed time; the local proxies in which time passes favoured our previous base (those targeting E10.5, the transition on which that base was tuned, and one targeting the external E14.5 sample), so this choice is a judgement, not a locally validated result. No leaderboard score entered the content of the prediction; the score of our first submission and the public leaderboard were used only to judge which local proxies to trust when choosing between complete candidate files.

External data and compliance: GEO GSE272154 / GSM8394736 (E14.5, wild type, 10x Multiome nuclei). No external data staged after E9.5 up to and including E13.5 was used or downloaded, and no pre-trained model was used. The bulk RNA-seq series of our first submission (GSE223237) is not used by this file.

AI involvement: the method was developed and run with AI coding agents (Claude Code) under the team's direction; the team chose which file to submit. Code is available on request.
