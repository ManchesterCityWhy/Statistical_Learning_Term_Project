Human Team. Composition-aware resampling of the last observed stage, mixed with real later-stage nuclei as a future anchor.

(1) Base population (unchanged from our previous method). Starting from the E10.5 cells of the official P3 validation release, a gene-level developmental trend is estimated from public bulk RNA-seq of mouse hearts (GEO GSE223237: E9.5 samples GSM6943750, GSM6943751, GSM6943752 and E14.5 samples GSM6943761, GSM6943762, GSM6943763; Ensembl IDs mapped to symbols with the gene table of GSM6211349 only, no expression values from it), shrunk towards zero by replicate agreement and expression level. A ridge-penalised non-negative least-squares deconvolution of E10.5 cell-type mean profiles finds cell-type proportions whose pseudobulk follows this trend; 2,000 E10.5 cells are resampled without replacement accordingly and the bulk trend is applied within cells. The pseudobulk of these cells is then moved to a gene-wise target change (a two-term linear combination of the bulk-implied and the deconvolution-implied change, weights fitted on the E9.5-to-E10.5 validation transition with E9.5 thinned to the E10.5 sequencing depth) by an additive shift on non-zero entries.

(2) Future anchor (new). 1,200 real nuclei from a public wild-type E14.5 mouse heart profiled with the same assay as the challenge data (10x Single Cell Multiome, gene-expression part, Cell Ranger ARC 2.0.2, mm10; GEO series GSE272154, sample GSM8394736 "RNA Wild Type e14.5 rep1") are added, so that they make up 37.5% of the 3,200 submitted cells. Only this wild-type sample was downloaded; the mutant samples of the series were not used. Nuclei called by Cell Ranger were kept if they had at least 1,000 UMIs, at least 500 detected genes and at most 25% mitochondrial UMIs, mapped to the official gene list by symbol, normalised to 10,000 counts and log1p-transformed; no other correction was applied.

The mixing fraction was fixed by a rule set on local validation only: with E9.5 or E8.5 as the source, E10.5 as the target and the same E14.5 anchor, the best fraction was about 0.75 to 0.9 times the elapsed-time fraction (0.15 for one fifth of the interval, 0.25 to 0.30 for one third); E12.5 lies halfway between E10.5 and E14.5, giving 0.375. All hyperparameters were selected on E10.5 using only E8.5, E9.5 and the external data as inputs; no leaderboard score entered the content of the prediction.

External data and compliance: GSE223237 (bulk, E9.5 and E14.5 samples listed above), GSM6211349 (gene annotation table only), GSE272154 / GSM8394736 (E14.5 single-nucleus Multiome, wild type). No external data staged after E9.5 up to and including E13.5 was used or downloaded, and no pre-trained model was used. Analysis code was written with AI assistance (Claude Code); the method and the submitted file were selected by the team.
