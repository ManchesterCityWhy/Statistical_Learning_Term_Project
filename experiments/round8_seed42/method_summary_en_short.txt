Developed and run with AI coding agents (Claude Code) under human direction; people read intermediate results and chose the file. 5,000 nuclei, all real E10.5 nuclei from the dissected-heart library of the official validation release, modified in place: (1) 55% drawn uniformly; (2) 45% drawn in the cell-type composition of a public wild-type E14.5 heart (GEO GSE272154 / GSM8394736, 10x Multiome, same Cell Ranger ARC version; QC thresholds only): 2,250 of its nuclei are picked, assigned to their nearest E10.5 cell type and each represented by a real E10.5 cell of that type; (3) genes a cell type barely expresses at E10.5 (below 0.3 per 10k) are mixed 75:25 at the molecule level with an E14.5 nucleus of the same type (other lineages' markers excluded; blood cells and non-heart cell types, 16% of the cells, are not mixed); (4) developmental shifts: a two-witness shift for the cardiomyocyte, endocardial and epicardial lineages (a gene moves towards E14.5 only if it also moved that way from E9.5 to E10.5 in the challenge data) and, for all cells, a within-cell bulk trend from public E9.5/E14.5 heart bulk RNA-seq (GEO GSE223237, E9.5 and E14.5 samples only; gene table of GSM6211349 for symbols). Settings were fixed on local proxies and simulated E12.5 populations. Scores returned for our first submission were used to choose the E14.5 share (0.45; 20 candidate files in two rounds: the smallest share that fits those per-metric scores as well as the best candidate of its group), as a check on the two-witness shift, and qualitatively to weight local settings; the public leaderboard (method names and scores of other entries) was read qualitatively and is one reason for step 3. No external data from after E9.5 up to and including E13.5; no pre-trained models. Code available on request.
