Developed and run with AI coding agents (Claude Code) under human direction: people set the goals, read intermediate results (including the scores returned for the first submission and the public test leaderboard) and chose which file to submit.

Prediction for E12.5 (T1:test): 5,000 nuclei. Every one of them is a real E10.5 nucleus of the official P3 validation release (10x library of the dissected heart, barcode suffix _3; 94% of its cells are heart or blood cell types, against 43-47% in the other two libraries), modified in place as described in steps 3 and 4 (4,997 distinct nuclei; three are used twice with independent draws in step 3).

1. Base (2,750 cells, 55%): drawn uniformly without replacement, natural composition.

2. Cells added in the E14.5 composition (2,250 cells, 45%). 2,250 nuclei are picked at random from a public wild-type E14.5 mouse heart (GEO GSE272154, sample GSM8394736; same assay, Cell Ranger ARC 2.0.2, mm10; QC thresholds only: >= 1000 UMI, >= 500 genes, <= 25% mitochondrial) and each is assigned to its nearest E10.5 cell type (correlation with type centroids). Each is then represented by a real E10.5 cell of the assigned type that is not used otherwise (same lineage if the type runs out). The E14.5 sample contributes the cell-type composition here, not cells.

3. Genes that switch on (all cells of lineages present in the E14.5 sample, about 84% of the file). For the genes a cell type barely expresses at E10.5 (type mean below 0.3 counts per 10k; 15,000-21,000 genes per type, 8-9% of its molecules), each cell is paired with a random E14.5 nucleus of its cell type; the cell keeps each of its own molecules in these genes with probability 0.75 (also in the few of them that are excluded from receiving, see below) and receives E14.5 molecules at 0.25 x its depth x the gene's share in the profile of that nucleus and its 19 nearest E14.5 nuclei (Poisson). Genes that another lineage expresses at least 20 times more strongly at E10.5 are removed from the profile first and are not received (they reach the E14.5 nuclei as ambient RNA). All other genes keep the cell's own counts. The share 0.25 is half of the midpoint between the two stages; it was the smaller of two values tried and was kept because it costs almost nothing if E12.5 shows no such change.

4. Developmental shifts, applied to all 5,000 cells. (a) Two-witness shift, for the cardiomyocyte, endocardial/endothelial and epicardial lineages (the other lineages lack one of the two witnesses and are not moved): within a lineage, a gene is moved towards its E14.5 level only if it also changed in the same direction from E9.5 to E10.5 inside the challenge data and is expressed by that lineage at E10.5; the log change is at most half of the E10.5-to-E14.5 log-ratio and at most two days at the E9.5-to-E10.5 rate. (b) Bulk trend, for all cells: a gene-level log-ratio from public bulk RNA-seq of E9.5 and E14.5 hearts (GEO GSE223237: GSM6943750-52 and GSM6943761-63; Ensembl IDs mapped to symbols with the gene table of GSM6211349, no expression values from it), shrunk by replicate agreement, applied within cells for two of its five days. Zeros stay zero in this step; every cell is renormalised to 10,000 counts and log1p-transformed.

Use of returned scores and of the public leaderboard. The scores returned for our first official submission (h1) were used in three ways. (i) The share of cells added in the E14.5 composition: for each of 20 candidate files (shares 0 to 0.60, also varying a position shift in the first round and the two-witness shift in the second; the second round was defined after the best candidate of the first lay at the edge of its grid) we computed locally what h1 would have scored had that candidate been the truth; in the second round, among the four candidates without the two-witness shift, we took the smallest share that fits the returned per-metric scores as well as the best of them (0.45; shares between 0.30 and 0.60 fit almost equally well; in these candidates all added cells were re-sequenced E14.5 nuclei, which the final file no longer contains). (ii) The same computation served as a check on the two-witness shift (candidates with it fit better). (iii) Qualitatively: h1's low score is why local settings with elapsed time were given more weight than a zero-elapsed setting when the other settings were fixed. The public test leaderboard was read qualitatively: entries whose method names state real E10.5 cells only (direction scores 79.9-83.6) and entries whose names state E10.5 + E14.5 mixtures (87.9-92.0) were compared with the same two kinds of file in local simulations, and this comparison is one of the reasons for step 3. No quantity derived from returned or public scores enters the file other than through these choices among alternatives built without them. The other settings were fixed on local proxies built from the released stages (targets split into a selection half and a confirmation half), on simulated E12.5 populations, or by criteria that use no score.

External data and exclusions. GSE272154/GSM8394736 (E14.5) and GSE223237 (E9.5 and E14.5 samples only; gene table of GSM6211349). No external data from after E9.5 up to and including E13.5; no pre-trained models. Code and logs available on request.
