Developed and run with AI coding agents (Claude Code) under human direction: people set the goals, read intermediate results (including the scores returned for the first submission) and chose which file to submit.

Prediction for E12.5 (T1:test): 5,000 nuclei placed between the E10.5 validation release and a public E14.5 heart.

1. Base (2,750 cells, 55%). E10.5 cells of the official P3 validation release, taken only from the 10x library of the dissected heart (barcode suffix _3; 94% of its cells are heart or blood cell types, against 43-47% in the other two libraries), drawn uniformly without replacement, natural composition.

2. E14.5 component (2,250 nuclei, 45%). Nuclei of a public wild-type E14.5 mouse heart (GEO GSE272154, sample GSM8394736), profiled with the same assay as the challenge data and aligned with the same Cell Ranger ARC version (2.0.2, mm10). We applied QC thresholds only (>= 1000 UMI, >= 500 genes, <= 25% mitochondrial); no ambient-RNA correction or doublet removal. The sample comes from another laboratory, is sequenced about 3.4 times shallower and carries ambient cardiomyocyte RNA, so the nuclei are not used as they are. Each selected nucleus is assigned to its nearest E10.5 cell type (correlation with type centroids); the raw counts of its 20 nearest E14.5 nuclei are summed; the profile is scaled gene by gene (by at most e^3) to bring the mean profile of every assigned cell type towards the E10.5 mean of that type (lineage level for rare types; nuclei assigned to non-heart types receive only the correction shared by all lineages); and a new nucleus is drawn from the profile (multinomial) at the UMI depth of an E10.5 heart-library cell of the same lineage. The aim is that the component contributes the E14.5 cell-type composition rather than between-laboratory differences in expression level. Measured: for heart cell types the type means end within about 7-15% (median over expressed genes) of the E10.5 means; for the nuclei assigned to non-heart types (9% of the component) the difference is only about halved and the ambient cardiomyocyte RNA remains; and the re-sequenced nuclei vary less within a cell type than real cells do (about half the variance in the leading principal components), a known limitation. The neighbourhood size (20) matches the number of detected genes of real E10.5 cells of the same depth.

3. Developmental shifts, applied to base cells and re-sequenced nuclei alike. (a) Two-witness shift, for the cardiomyocyte, endocardial/endothelial and epicardial lineages (about 72% of the cells; the other lineages lack one of the two witnesses and are not moved): within a lineage, a gene is moved towards its E14.5 level only if it also changed in the same direction from E9.5 to E10.5 inside the challenge data and is expressed by that lineage at E10.5; the log change is at most half of the E10.5-to-E14.5 log-ratio and at most two days at the E9.5-to-E10.5 rate. (b) Bulk trend, for all cells: a gene-level log-ratio from public bulk RNA-seq of E9.5 and E14.5 hearts (GEO GSE223237: GSM6943750-52 and GSM6943761-63; Ensembl IDs mapped to symbols with the gene table of GSM6211349, no expression values from it), shrunk by replicate agreement, applied within cells for two of its five days. Zeros stay zero; every cell is renormalised to 10,000 counts and log1p-transformed.

Use of returned scores. The scores returned for our first official submission (h1) were used in three ways. (i) The share of the E14.5 component: for each of 20 candidate files (shares 0 to 0.60, also varying a position shift in the first round and the two-witness shift in the second; the second round was defined after the best candidate of the first lay at the edge of its grid) we computed locally what h1 would have scored had that candidate been the truth; in the second round, among the four candidates without the two-witness shift, we took the smallest share that fits the returned per-metric scores as well as the best of them (0.45; shares between 0.30 and 0.60 fit almost equally well). (ii) The same computation served as a check on the two-witness shift (candidates with it fit better). (iii) Qualitatively: h1's low score is why local settings with elapsed time were given more weight than a zero-elapsed setting when the other settings were fixed, and why this method was preferred to our earlier one. No quantity derived from returned scores enters the file other than through these choices among alternatives built without them. The other settings were fixed on local proxies built from the released stages (targets split into a selection half and a confirmation half) or by criteria that use no score.

External data and exclusions. GSE272154/GSM8394736 (E14.5) and GSE223237 (E9.5 and E14.5 samples only; gene table of GSM6211349). No external data from after E9.5 up to and including E13.5; no pre-trained models. Code and logs available on request.
