Developed and run with AI coding agents (Claude Code) under human direction; people read intermediate results and chose the file. 5,000 nuclei: (1) 55% real E10.5 cells from the dissected-heart library of the official validation release, natural composition; (2) 45% cells added in the cell-type composition of a public wild-type E14.5 heart (GEO GSE272154 / GSM8394736, 10x Multiome, same Cell Ranger ARC version; QC thresholds only): 2,250 of its nuclei are picked and assigned to their nearest E10.5 cell type; half are represented by a real E10.5 cell of that type, the other half are re-sequenced nuclei (20 nearest neighbours pooled, scaled to bring every heart cell type's mean towards the E10.5 mean of that type, re-drawn at E10.5 sequencing depth; these vary less within a type than real cells, which is why half were replaced); (3) developmental shifts: a two-witness shift for the cardiomyocyte, endocardial and epicardial lineages (a gene moves towards E14.5 only if it also moved that way from E9.5 to E10.5 in the challenge data) and, for all cells, a within-cell bulk trend from public E9.5/E14.5 heart bulk RNA-seq (GEO GSE223237, E9.5 and E14.5 samples only; gene table of GSM6211349 for symbols). Settings were fixed on local proxies with selection/confirmation halves. Scores returned for our first submission were used to choose the E14.5 share (0.45; 20 candidate files in two rounds, shares 0-0.60: the smallest share that fits those per-metric scores as well as the best candidate of its group), as a check on the two-witness shift, and qualitatively (with the public leaderboard) to weight local settings. No external data from after E9.5 up to and including E13.5; no pre-trained models. Code available on request.
